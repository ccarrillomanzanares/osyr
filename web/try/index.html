TRY OSYR

This opens the OSYR assistant on a pretend laptop, in your browser. Ask it things in your own language: what wifi networks are around, to lower the volume, to find a document, how much disk is left.

  • The laptop is not real: its wifi, sound, displays and Bluetooth are simulated, and nothing reaches the Internet.
  • What you get here is the assistant's console. The desktop, the voice and looking at the screen need the real system.
  • The model runs on our server, not on your computer, and it is shared: answers take a while, and there may be a queue.
  • A session lasts 15 minutes. Everything is erased when it ends.
  • OSYR is in development and its AI makes mistakes. Do not type personal data.
